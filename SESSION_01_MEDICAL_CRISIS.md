## #scene: The Ambulance Arrives - July 6, 2025, 07:12 PST

**Location**: Blake residence, San Rafael

The FRMC ambulance is unmarked. Dark blue, professional, completely anonymous. Alexander is driving. Aria is already reviewing something on a tablet in the back, one hand resting on her visibly pregnant belly.

Aria's expression tightens. "May we?"

Collin leads them back. Alexander brings the medical kit and a portable resonance monitor.

Sciara is sitting on the edge of the bed, hunched forward slightly, one hand pressed to her temple. She's wearing soft clothes—sweats, an oversized cardigan. The collar is visible at her throat, matte black against pale skin. She looks up when they enter, and her eyes are wrong. Confused. Frightened. Not tracking quite right.

"Hi, Sciara," Aria says gently, kneeling down to be at eye level. "I'm Dr. Shahrazai from Falling River. Your husband called us because you're not feeling well. We're going to take you somewhere safe where we can help. Is that okay?"

Sciara blinks slowly. "I—I didn't mean to. I've been trying. I'm trying so hard."

"I know," Aria says, and there's something in her voice—absolute certainty, compassion without pity. "You're not in trouble. We're going to help you feel better."

Sciara nods, but she's trembling. "Am I going back to Site-17?"

"No," Collin says quietly, moving to sit beside her. Not crowding, just close. Present. "Falling River. Remember? We talked about this."

His hand finds hers. Gentle. Grounding. The subtle shift in Sciara's breathing is almost imperceptible—some of the panic easing, just slightly, at the familiar structure of his presence.

"Okay," Sciara whispers. "Okay. I trust you."

Aria exchanges a glance with Alexander. He's already setting up the portable monitor, but his expression is grim.

"Let's get her loaded," Aria says. "I want to run full diagnostics en route."

Dark spots where resonance should be. Where brain activity should be. Just... nothing.

Because they shouldn't happen at all in a properly administered program.

"Everything," Mira says, and her voice cracks. "I can see everything. I shouldn't be able to see her aura at all without a resonance monitor, but I can see—there are holes, Ava. There are fucking holes."
