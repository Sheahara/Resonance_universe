## Core Principles

### Consent as Mechanism
In the Resonance framework, consent has measurable biochemical and psionic effects. An identical compound drawn under duress is "flat" or unstable; if taken voluntarily (or even just believed to be voluntary), the resonance structure locks in cleanly and amplifies efficacy. FRMC in particular recognizes this and codifies it into their pharmacology—sometimes ritualized to make sure the donor believes their contribution was a gift.

### Non-Sapient Entity Consent
With non-sapient organisms, this gets ethically strange: how do you define "consent" from a coral, a dog, or a mycelial network? FRMC researchers argue about this constantly. The effectiveness of resonance-active compounds often provides empirical evidence of whether proper energetic exchange occurred.

### Institutional Approaches
- **ICR**: Molecular pharmacology, nanoparticle delivery, clinical sterility, "borrowed anchors"
- **FRMC**: Ritual pharmacology, biological symbiosis, consent-based harvesting, patient-specific formulations
- **TPC**: Archetypal infusions, collective memory work, "greater good" justifications, communal compounds
- **Prometheus/PPTL**: Corner-cutting, untested prototypes, black box formulas, exploitable designs

### Hybridization
Resonance medicine almost always works best when hybridized: molecular pharmacology layered with psionic resonance tuning, patient-specific genetic or EVE signatures, and sometimes ritual field stabilization.
