## Eyewitness Testimony (Unrecorded)

### What Everyone Knows
Sevron placed Sciara's collar herself on March 31, 2026. She explained this as appropriate given Sciara's position with the agency and the "complex nature of the breach." Most found it slightly unusual but rationalized it - Sciara was seizing, Sevron was the medical director.

### What Mira Saw That Others Didn't (March 31, 2026)

"Everyone knows Sevron placed that collar herself—she made a whole thing about it being appropriate given Sciara's position, the complexity of the breach. But I saw something they didn't. She was already in the room when I got there. Said she'd been passing by and heard the alarm. But she had the collar *with* her. Already calibrated. Already keyed to Sciara's resonance signature. You don't just have those lying around. You don't just *happen* to have one ready, pre-configured for a specific person."

### Why Mira Couldn't Tell Anyone

"I've been living on-site at ICR for two years, sleeping down the hall from that woman's office, and I couldn't figure out who to tell or what to even say. 'The medical director had a collar ready before the emergency happened?' It sounds paranoid. It sounds crazy."

### Implication

The containment breach was likely staged or anticipated. Sevron had a collar prepared specifically for Sciara before any emergency occurred. This is evidence of **pre-planning**, not just unusual protocol - and that's what's damning.
