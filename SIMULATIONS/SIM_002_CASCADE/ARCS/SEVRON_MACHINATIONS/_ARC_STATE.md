## The Collar Lockout Problem

The collar refusing to respond to FRMC attending physician override is itself evidence of interference. Standard SAFE devices are designed to accept authorized medical override from any credentialed physician. A device that refuses override has been modified to restrict access.

Sevron may not realize this cuts both ways:
- **Her perspective:** They can't get into the device, can't see what's inside
- **Legal reality:** A medical device that refuses to allow a physician to treat a patient is evidence of unauthorized modification. This fact alone supports the warrant application.
