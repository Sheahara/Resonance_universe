## Arc Summary

This arc follows Ava Kessler's dangerous position as the whistleblower who defied Director Sevron to save Sciara Blake. It tracks her attempts to document the truth, protect her family, navigate TPC connections, and ultimately extract herself from ICR before Sevron can retaliate.

**Core Themes:**
- The cost of doing right
- Family as vulnerability and motivation
- Institutional retaliation
- Trust in impossible situations

**POV Sources:**
- Kessler's personal encrypted notes
- Covert communications with TPC
- ICR surveillance (what they see)
- Family moments (humanizing)
