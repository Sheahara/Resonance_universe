## Quick Summary

Sciara Blake is recovering from three months of systematic harm via a weaponized SAFE collar. This arc follows her physical healing, cognitive rehabilitation, emotional processing, and evolving relationship with Collin as he learns to care for a version of his wife he doesn't fully recognize.

**Recovery is measured in weeks and months, not hours and days.**
