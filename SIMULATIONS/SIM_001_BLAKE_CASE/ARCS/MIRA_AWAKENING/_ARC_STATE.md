## Arc Summary

This arc follows Mira Fallon, a SAFE participant who has been in the program for three years. She witnessed Sciara's collapse and begins to question the program that has conditioned her to believe she needs it. This arc explores the psychological cost of deprogramming and the danger of awakening while still collared.

**Core Themes:**
- Conditioning and its unraveling
- The terror of questioning your cage
- Solidarity between survivors
- Hope as dangerous rebellion

**POV Sources:**
- Mira's internal observations (limited by conditioning)
- SAFE program documentation about her
- Other participant perspectives
- Medical/behavioral records
