## Quick Summary

Mira Fallon has been in the SAFE program for three years. She witnessed Sciara's collapse and is beginning to question the program that has conditioned her to believe she needs it. This arc explores awakening while still collared—dangerous and potentially fatal.

**She tried to kill herself rather than continue. She still believes she needs the program.**
