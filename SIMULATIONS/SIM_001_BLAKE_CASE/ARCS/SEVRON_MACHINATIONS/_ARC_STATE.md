## Arc Summary

This arc tracks Dr. Talia Sevron's response to the Blake crisis—her institutional defense, evidence manipulation, counter-investigations, and escalating measures to protect herself and the SAFE program. The player experiences this through intercepted communications, leaked documents, and observed consequences.

**Core Themes:**
- Institutional power vs. individual truth
- The banality of bureaucratic evil
- Self-justification and rationalization
- The cornered predator becomes dangerous

**POV Sources:**
